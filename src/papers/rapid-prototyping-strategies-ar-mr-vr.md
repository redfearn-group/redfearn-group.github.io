Teams building augmented, mixed, and virtual reality systems repeatedly over-invest in high-fidelity prototypes before they have answered the questions a paper sketch would have settled in an afternoon. This talk works through which rapid design and prototyping methods actually earn their cost at each stage of an immersive project, drawn from the thirteen head-worn display experiments behind the [dissertation](/publications/rapid-design-prototyping-mixed-reality/).

Presented at the UXPA International Conference in Scottsdale, Arizona, June 2019. The slide deck is posted here in full.
